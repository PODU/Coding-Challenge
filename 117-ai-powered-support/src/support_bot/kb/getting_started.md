# Getting Started

To begin, create a free account on the Coding Challenges platform and pick a
challenge from the catalogue. Beginners are encouraged to start with the
"Build your own wc" challenge, which rebuilds the Unix word-count tool and
teaches command-line argument parsing and reading from standard input.

Every challenge starts with Step 0, an environment-setup step where you choose
your language and verify your toolchain works. From there you work through the
numbered steps in order, writing tests as you go.

You do not need to finish a challenge in one sitting. Your progress is saved to
your account, so you can pause and resume any challenge at any time.
