# Frequently Asked Questions

## How much does it cost?
Creating an account and accessing the core challenges is free. A paid Pro
subscription unlocks bonus challenges, official solutions, and detailed
step-by-step guides.

## Which programming languages are supported?
All of them. Challenges are language-agnostic — solve them in Python,
TypeScript, Go, Java, Rust, C, or anything else. The platform never checks your
code in a specific language; you verify your own solution with the tests you
write.

## Do I have to follow the steps in order?
Yes, we recommend it. Each step builds on the previous one, so following them in
order gives the smoothest learning curve. Advanced users can skip ahead, but the
incremental path is designed to teach the underlying concepts.

## How do I reset my progress on a challenge?
Open the challenge page, click "Settings", and choose "Reset progress". This
clears your saved steps for that challenge so you can start it again from
Step 0. Your account and other challenges are unaffected.

## Can I get help when I'm stuck?
Yes. Each challenge has a community discussion thread, and Pro members can view
the official reference solution for every step.
